Deckmate

A beginner-friendly two-deck DJ mixer, right in your browser.

How to mix in 4 steps
  1. Paste YouTube links, IDs, or a playlist link into the library, or use the starter tracks.
  2. Load one track to Deck A and another to Deck B.
  3. Hit play on Deck A. Use tempo and tap-BPM to feel out the beat before bringing in Deck B.
  4. Move the crossfader from A to B (or hit "Mix to B") to blend the two together.

Library

Why no Spotify?

Spotify only allows full-track playback through a Premium-login, OAuth-based player, which can't be wired into a shared demo like this one. YouTube works with no login needed, so that's the engine here — search the song on YouTube and paste that link instead.
